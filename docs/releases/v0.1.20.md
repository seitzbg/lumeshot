One change, refining the look of the annotation editor's tool list.

## The editing tools now read as a native sidebar

The tool rail on the left of the editor has been reworked to look and feel like a
standard macOS sidebar. The tools are organised under quiet section headers —
**Shapes** (Select, Rectangle, Ellipse, Line, Arrow, Freehand), **Redact** (Blur,
Pixelate) and **Annotate** (Text, Highlighter, Step, Crop) — the sidebar carries
the system's translucent material, and the active tool is shown as a full,
accent-coloured row the way selection looks in Finder and Mail.

Nothing about what the tools do has changed; the list is just easier to scan and
more at home on macOS.
