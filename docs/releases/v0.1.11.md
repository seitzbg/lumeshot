- **Check for Updates…** can now download the update for you. It fetches the dmg, checks
  it against the checksum published with the release, saves it to your Downloads folder,
  and offers to reveal it in Finder. You still open it and drag Lumeshot to Applications —
  it does not install itself.
- Removing an upload from History now clears its status. Deleting a failed upload used to
  leave the menu-bar warning icon and the History banner still reporting a failure for an
  entry that was already gone.
