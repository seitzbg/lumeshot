Capture delivery fixes, the completed Lumeshot rebrand, and improved Settings behavior.

- Fix region/window capture and editor Save/Upload silently skipping persistence.
- Copy the captured image to the clipboard; after a successful upload, copy the image URL. Skip URL replacement when a newer copy is detected. Cross-app clipboard preservation is best effort.
- Configure multiple uploaders and choose one active uploader. Adding another preserves the selection; removing the active uploader disables automatic upload.
- Show Settings in the Dock and app switcher while open, and restore minimized Settings when clicking the Dock icon.
- Use the Lumeshot identity throughout the app, storage paths, signing scripts, and documentation.

### Upgrading

This is a clean break in application identity. Existing application data is left untouched and is not automatically migrated. Configure upload destinations and credentials again, and grant Screen Recording and notification permissions to the new app identity.
