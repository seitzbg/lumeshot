One fix, to something you would have seen every time you clicked a notification.

## Clicking a notification no longer opens Settings

Clicking **Capture complete** revealed the screenshot in Finder and then put the
Settings window in front of it. Clicking **Uploaded** opened the link and did the
same. Both notifications were doing the right thing — the Settings window was
uninvited, and it covered the thing you had just asked for.

macOS reports the app being activated by a notification click the same way it
reports a click on the Dock icon, and Lumeshot opens Settings for the latter
because a menu-bar app has no other window to show. It now tells the two apart.

Clicking the Dock icon still opens Settings, including straight after dismissing
a notification.
