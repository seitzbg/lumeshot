One change: the editor now opens sized to the capture.

## The editor window fits what you captured

Instead of opening at a fixed size, the editor now sizes its window to the capture
itself. A capture opens showing the image at 1:1 — the window is the image's
on-screen size (its pixels divided by the display's scale factor) plus the tool
rail and top bar. A capture larger than your display is capped to fit the screen
(the canvas scales it down to show all of it), and a very small capture still
opens at a comfortable minimum rather than a tiny window.

This replaces the remembered-size behaviour from v0.1.20/v0.1.21: the size now
follows each capture rather than reusing the last one. The window remains freely
resizable once open.
